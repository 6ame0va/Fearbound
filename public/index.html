How to play

Find the truth. Expose the lie.

  1. Join a room with 3–8 players. The host selects the difficulty and begins.
  2. Read your private clue. One player is secretly the Deceiver and receives a false theory.
  3. Discuss the clues. Investigators work out what happened; the Deceiver tries to redirect them.
  4. Before time runs out, choose the true theory and accuse the Deceiver.
  5. Investigators earn one point for each correct choice. The Deceiver earns two points if the group fails to solve the case and expose them.
